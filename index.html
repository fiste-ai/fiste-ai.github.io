What Suseli is being built to do

A text-to-speech model for the Nepali language, made in Nepal.

Natural Nepali

स्वाभाविक नेपाली

Clear pronunciation and the rhythm of spoken Nepali, including the rise at the end of a question.

Expressive

भावसहितको बोली

Tell it how to speak: calm, cheerful, serious or sad.

Many voices

धेरै आवाज

Built-in voices, and a new voice from a short recording, only with the speaker's consent.

Small and fast

सानो र छिटो

A compact version that runs on a laptop processor or a phone, without a server.

The name

Two small sounds of Nepal, and the mountains they come from.

सुसेली (suseli) is a tune you whistle.

फिस्टो, also called फिस्टे (fiste), is the name for the small warbler birds of Nepal. One of them, the सुसेली फिस्टो, is the Whistler's warbler.

Our mark is the head of a fiste over the Himalaya: a ring for the head, and the mountains in brush strokes.

Responsible use

A voice belongs to a person. Suseli will create a new voice only from recordings the user has the right to use, and its terms will forbid impersonation and fraud.
